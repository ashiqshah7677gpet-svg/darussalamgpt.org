Our Mission & Vision

Established to bring the light of knowledge to those who seek it, Darussalam has been a beacon of hope and education for the community.

We blend traditional Islamic teachings with modern empowerment skills. From nurturing young minds in our Madrasa to providing livelihood skills in our Ladies Tailoring Institute, our goal is holistic community development grounded in faith.
